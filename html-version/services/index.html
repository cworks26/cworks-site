Website Building

End-to-end web development — from a blank page to a live, polished site.

What's included

  • DesignWe start with your goals, brand, and inspiration. From there we put together a visual layout (colours, typography, and structure) and share it with you for feedback before a single line of code is written.
  • DevelopmentYour site is built in modern, standards-based code — no bloat. Every site is built mobile-first, tested across browsers, and optimised for fast load times.

Questions

Answers before you ask.

How long does a project take?

Starter sites take around 1 week, Standard sites 2–3 weeks, and larger systems are scoped per project. Content readiness and revision rounds affect delivery.

Do I need to provide the content?

Yes — you provide your text, images, and branding. We advise on structure and sourcing, and copywriting is available as a paid add-on.

Can you update or rebuild my existing site?

Yes. Share your current site and what you'd like changed, and we'll assess and quote from there.

What do I need to get started?

Just a rough idea of what you want, a few sites you like the look of, and your budget. We handle the rest from the discovery call onwards.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes. Post-launch support is available for updates, fixes, and small changes — either case-by-case or as a monthly retainer.

What about payment terms?

50% deposit to begin, balance due on launch. Hosting is free for the first year, then billed annually via Mobile Money.
